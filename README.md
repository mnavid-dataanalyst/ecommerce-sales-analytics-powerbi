## 🧐 Why I Built This

Most e-commerce teams sit on mountains of sales data but never turn it into decisions. This project is my attempt to change that — a fully interactive **Power BI dashboard** that digs into what's really driving (and quietly draining) revenue for an online retail business.

Instead of just charting numbers, every page is built around a *question* a business leader would actually ask: *Is discounting hurting our margins? Why is one region always late on delivery? Which product category is secretly underperforming?* The dashboard answers each one with visuals, not guesswork.

If you're a recruiter, hiring manager, or fellow analyst browsing GitHub for **Power BI portfolio projects**, **e-commerce analytics dashboards**, or **data analyst case studies** — this one's built to be read like a story, not just clicked through like a report.

## 🔑 Key Insights the Dashboard Uncovers

- 💰 **Revenue has plateaued** across 2022–2024, signaling limited organic growth and a need for new demand drivers.
- 🏷️ **Discounting doesn't buy loyalty.** Customer satisfaction stays flat (~2.9–3.0) regardless of discount size — but the **0–10% discount tier generates 3x more revenue** than the 31%+ tier. Heavy discounting is quietly eating margins for nothing in return.
- 🛍️ **Electronics is the revenue engine**, contributing over **35% of total revenue** ($1.83M), while Beauty lags as the smallest category (~15%).
- 🚚 **Delivery speed drives satisfaction.** Orders delivered within 5 days consistently earn higher ratings — yet every region misses the on-time benchmark, with the **West region facing the longest delays**.
- 🌍 **Regional performance is closely balanced**, with the West narrowly leading in both order volume and revenue share, but also lagging in delivery reliability.
- 💳 **Cash payments correlate with higher order values** compared to other payment methods.
